## Design Context

### Users
Mixed cross-functional team — designers, developers, and PMs capturing page snapshots and remixing them collaboratively. Used as a Chrome sidebar during active work sessions.

### Brand Personality
Fast, utilitarian, no-nonsense. A power tool that stays efficient and gets out of the way. Speed and clarity over decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
Apple-inspired light theme with indigo (#6366f1) accent. System font stack. Minimal shadows, clean borders, frosted-glass header. The existing direction is solid — polish should tighten consistency, not reinvent.

### Design Principles
1. **Speed over spectacle** — every pixel should serve function, not decoration
2. **Consistency over novelty** — spacing, type, and color should follow the established token system
3. **Clarity over cleverness** — labels, states, and actions should be immediately obvious
